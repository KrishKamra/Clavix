# Code of conduct

Clavix adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
By taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews or discussions, you agree to follow it.

## In short

We want Clavix to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or
identity. In practice that means:

| We do | We don't |
|-------|----------|
| Assume good faith and ask before we judge | Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately |
| Critique code and ideas, not people | Make sexualised, violent or discriminatory remarks |
| Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully | Publish anyone's private information without consent |
| Credit other people's work | Keep pushing after someone has asked us to stop |
| Own our mistakes and learn from them | Use the project to attack or exclude others |

Accessibility is part of why Clavix exists (it lets people operate a computer without their hands),
so we ask contributors to keep accessibility in mind in both the software and the conversation.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions) and wherever someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through
**[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/KrishKamra/Clavix/security/advisories/new)** or by
messaging a maintainer listed in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md). Every report is reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences
decided by the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, chosen according to the impact of the behaviour.
